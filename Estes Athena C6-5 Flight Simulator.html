Estes Athena (2452) · C6-5 flight simulator

2D point-mass model with drag, wind, burn-time mass loss and a parachute that opens at ejection.

PoweredCoastParachute

Model: m·dv/dt = T − mg − ½ρCdA|vrel|vrel. Thrust curve is an approximate C6 shape scaled to exactly 10 N·s (peak 15.3 N, 1.6 s). The 15.9 g lost between the 69.2 g and 53.3 g masses (propellant 12.48 g plus ejection charge and wadding) is removed in proportion to the impulse delivered. ρ = 1.225 kg/m³. Thrust follows the launch rod for the first metre, then the airflow direction (weathercocking).
